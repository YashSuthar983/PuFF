# Cliff surface maps

**Rock Face 03** by Dario Barresi (photography) and Rico Cilliers (processing), Poly Haven, CC0.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license (CC0, no attribution required; credited anyway)

The 1k PNG maps (diffuse × AO, OpenGL normal, roughness, displacement) were converted to WebP by `tools/convert-rock.py`. The shader desaturates the albedo to wet black basalt and uses its dark crevice lines to place the green fissures.

Backup: `../rock-generated/` is our own procedural set from `tools/generate-rock-texture.py`, loaded automatically if these maps fail.
